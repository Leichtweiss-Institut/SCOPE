description=Generate orthogonal grid files for XBeach and AeoLiS coastal models from raster bathymetry and vector layers.
about=Interactive QGIS tool for generating model-ready, orthogonal grid files for coastal and geomorphological workflows. Draw and rotate a model extent on the map, then export x.grd, y.grd and z.grd from raster bathymetry, custom value grids (e.g. vegetation veg.grd, hard layers ne_layer.grd, bed friction bedfriction.grd) from polygon layers, and complex-valued threshold mask grids for AeoLiS-style masking. Supports adaptive cross-shore resolution, local or world coordinate output, model-area splitting and batch processing of multiple rasters.
